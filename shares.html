Sample Calculations

Formula: Founder gross = Payment × Founder %. Deductions = Withdraw fee + Platform fee + Savings/Reserve + Deposit fee. Deposit fee = Deposit % × gross revenue from property sales (sum of each sold property's price). Founder net = Founder gross − Deductions. Financer and Marketeer receive Payment × their respective %. Withdraw fee follows the ioTec/Centipid tier schedule based on total net withdrawals when enabled, then is deducted from that total; otherwise the fixed value applies. A negative founder net means the deductions exceed the founder's share for that payment amount.
